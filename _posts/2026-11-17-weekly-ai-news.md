A busy week: new frontier models from Anthropic and Google, AI agents that can pay for things, a lawsuit asking a court to pause frontier AI, and a leadership shock at Nvidia.

## Top story: Nvidia's CEO steps down

On November 12, Nvidia announced that founder Jensen Huang had stepped down as president, CEO and board member, effective immediately, after 33 years running the company. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO, and Harvey C. Jones becomes Nvidia's first board chair. Nvidia said it would still report third-quarter results on November 17 as planned.

Why it matters: Nvidia makes most of the chips that train and run today's AI models, and Huang was its public face. The question now is whether the change affects Nvidia's strategy, or how investors see the AI chip market. ([Nvidia](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition))

## Also this week

- **Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 (Nov 9).** Anthropic says the new model is up to 30% faster than Fable 5.1 and better at coding, reasoning and vision. It's on the Claude apps, the API, AWS, Google Cloud and Azure. A sibling, Claude Mythos 5.2, is the same model with looser safeguards for vetted cybersecurity and life-sciences organisations. ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-upgrades-claude-with-new-fable-5-2-model-details-here/), [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/anthropic-fable-coding-upgrade/))
- **Google ships Gemini 4 Hydrogen and widens Gemini 4 Carbon.** Hydrogen, a fast model aimed at everyday coding and agent tasks, became generally available on November 12. Carbon, Google's most capable model so far, is rolling out more widely to developers and businesses after a limited launch. ([Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/google-rolls-out-gemini-4-carbon-to-general-public-9b41bcb4))
- **OpenAI launches ChatGPT Wallet (Nov 13).** ChatGPT's "dots" agents can now buy things for you. You approve each purchase, and the agent never sees your real card number. It starts with US ChatGPT Pro users, with Visa, Mastercard and American Express as partners. ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/))
- **Nonprofits sue to pause frontier AI (Nov 12).** Four groups, including Public Citizen and the Alliance for Secure AI, sued Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceX's AI unit in San Francisco. They aren't asking for money. They want a court to block more capable models until those models can be shown to be safely controlled. The legal argument hasn't been tested in court. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/us-nonprofits-sue-tech-giants-to-halt-ai-development))
- **Alibaba releases Qwen4 Max (Nov 16).** It's the newest flagship in Alibaba's Qwen family, one of the leading model lines from China. ([LLM Gateway](https://llmgateway.io/timeline))

## Worth trying

If you're in the US on ChatGPT Pro, try **ChatGPT Wallet** on a small, low-stakes purchase. It's an early look at how "agentic shopping" might work.
